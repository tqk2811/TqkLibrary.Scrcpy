Copyright (c) the FFmpeg developers; their copyright notices are retained.

The FFmpeg DLLs come, unmodified, from the NuGet packages
`TqkLibrary.FFmpeg.Lgpl2.Native.Win.x64` / `.x86`. Those builds are configured
WITHOUT `--enable-gpl` and contain neither x264 nor x265; this library uses
FFmpeg for decoding and pixel/sample format conversion only.

`TqkLibrary.ScrcpyNative.dll` links avcodec, avutil, swresample and swscale.
The remaining DLLs of those packages (avformat, avdevice, avfilter) are shipped
alongside them and are covered by the same license.

Build scripts and configuration:  https://github.com/tqk2811/FFmpegBuild
Prebuilt binaries and sources:    https://github.com/tqk2811/FFmpegBuild/releases
FFmpeg upstream source:           https://ffmpeg.org/download.html

The native helper `TqkLibrary.ScrcpyNative.dll` links FFmpeg dynamically: the
FFmpeg DLLs are shipped as separate files (`runtimes/win-<rid>/native/`) and are
never linked statically. You are therefore free to replace them with your own
build of the same FFmpeg version, as permitted by LGPL-2.1 section 6.
